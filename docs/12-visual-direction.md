# NOVA — Visual Direction

## Identity
Futuristic, functional, restrained.

## Palette
- black
- graphite
- deep blue
- cold blue/cyan accents
- restrained pale highlights

## Architecture
Buildings should feel engineered rather than ornamental:
- simple masses
- clear entrances
- modular structures
- controlled emissive details
- infrastructure visibly integrated

## Transport lines
Luminous blue lines represent real transport connections. They should be derived from a network/state model.

They are not decorative trails.

## Visual state
World appearance should communicate simulation state:
- construction
- operational
- occupied
- active network
- unavailable service
- shortage

## Avoid
- giant gradients
- excessive bloom
- cyberpunk clutter
- random holograms
- decorative roads with no network meaning
