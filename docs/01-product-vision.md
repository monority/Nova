# NOVA — Product Vision

## Vision
NOVA is a visual colony/city simulator where a small number of understandable rules produce believable settlement growth.

The player should be able to look at the world and understand **why** something exists.

## Product pillars
1. Causality
2. Readability
3. Determinism
4. Infrastructure first
5. Minimalism
6. Visual feedback
7. Progressive complexity

## Experience
The experience starts almost empty. The player establishes infrastructure, creates a viable place to live, attracts colonists and gradually creates a functioning settlement.

The game should feel closer to operating a living system than arranging disconnected decorative objects.

## Explicit non-goals for the foundation
- giant technology tree
- dozens of resources
- complex politics
- vehicles as collectibles
- decorative city spam
- arbitrary population growth
- procedural complexity without gameplay value

Original mechanics can come later. First establish a sound simulation.
