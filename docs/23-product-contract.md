# NOVA — Product Contract

NOVA is considered healthy when:

1. A player can understand the immediate cause of major outcomes.
2. Buildings have meaningful operational states.
3. Population does not appear without a reason.
4. Services have providers and consumers.
5. Resources have producers and consumers.
6. Time changes simulation state deterministically.
7. Save/load preserves canonical state.
8. Rendering reflects simulation state rather than inventing it.
9. Complexity increases only when previous systems justify it.
10. The world remains visually legible.

This contract is more important than any individual feature list.
