# NOVA — Product Vision

> **Build a civilization. Watch it become something else.**

NOVA is a contemplative, systemic city-builder where the player establishes the conditions for a civilization to emerge rather than manually placing every building.

## Core Principle

> **The player draws the rules. The simulation draws the city.**

## Pillars

- Emergence over micromanagement
- Deterministic simulation
- Strong visual progression
- City as a living system
- Observation as gameplay

## Civilization Arc

```text
Wilderness
→ Settlement
→ Village
→ Town
→ City
→ Metropolis
→ Autonomous Civilization
```

## Visual Identity

NOVA is a living digital architectural model:

- dark negative space;
- simple geometric buildings;
- luminous infrastructure;
- controlled color;
- subtle particles and flows;
- minimal interface;
- no generic cyberpunk HUD;
- no photorealistic city simulation.

## Core Systems

```text
World
 ├── Terrain
 ├── City
 │    ├── Zones
 │    ├── Buildings
 │    ├── Roads
 │    └── Infrastructure
 ├── Population
 ├── Economy
 ├── Technology
 └── Events
```

## MVP

The measurable MVP scope is defined in [20-product-contract.md](./20-product-contract.md).

The first playable version contains:

- deterministic world;
- terrain;
- camera;
- roads;
- residential zones;
- procedural houses;
- population;
- food;
- energy;
- time;
- day/night;
- growth;
- save/load.

The first milestone is not feature breadth. It is a small city that is genuinely satisfying to watch evolve.
