# NOVA — Economy Foundation

## Recommended order
1. Colonist exists.
2. Colonist has a residence.
3. Colonist can eventually work.
4. Work produces income.
5. Income enables consumption.
6. Consumption creates demand.
7. Businesses/production answer demand.
8. Production creates jobs and consumes inputs.

## Avoid premature complexity
Do not begin with:
- stock markets
- debt
- taxation trees
- dozens of commodities
- dynamic prices everywhere
- financial speculation

A stable household/work/production loop is the foundation.

## Scarcity
Scarcity should be represented by a measurable shortage, not an arbitrary red warning.
